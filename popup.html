AI-Powered Email Enhancement

Extension Active

The extension is ready to polish your emails. Open Gmail and start composing!

📝 How to Use

  1. Open Gmail and click Compose
  2. Write your email draft (can be casual or rough)
  3. Click the "Polish with AI" button in the toolbar
  4. Watch as AI enhances your email to be more professional!
⚠️ Backend Required Make sure the backend server is running on localhost:3000. Check the README for setup instructions.
